## 🚀 Quick Start (Launch in 2 Days)

### Day 1: Setup (2-3 hours)

**Step 1: Deploy Website** (5 minutes)
1. Go to [netlify.com](https://netlify.com) and sign up
2. Drag and drop this entire folder
3. Your site is live at `yourname.netlify.app`
4. Optional: Connect custom domain later

**Step 2: Set Up Gumroad** (10 minutes)
1. Create account at [gumroad.com](https://gumroad.com)
2. Go to Settings → Payouts
3. Add your Belgian bank account (IBAN)
4. Create 5 products:
   - Budget Mastery Pack - $12
   - Productivity Power Pack - $15
   - Wellness & Self-Care Bundle - $12
   - Student Success Pack - $10
   - Complete Collection - $29

**Step 3: Update Payment Links** (5 minutes)
1. Open `script.js` in this folder
2. Replace the Gumroad URLs with your actual product links
3. Re-upload to Netlify (drag & drop again)

**Step 4: Create the Printables** (Choose one):

**OPTION A: Hire on Fiverr** ($30-50, 3-5 days, ZERO work for you)
1. Go to Fiverr.com
2. Search "printable planner design"
3. Find seller with 4.9+ stars
4. Message: "I need 69 printable planner pages. I have detailed specs. $40 for all?"
5. Send them `printables/CREATE-PRINTABLES-GUIDE.md`
6. Wait for delivery
7. **Your work: Literally none**

**OPTION B: DIY in Canva** (4-6 hours, $0, some effort)
1. Open `printables/CREATE-PRINTABLES-GUIDE.md`
2. Follow step-by-step Canva instructions
3. Use existing Canva templates (fastest)
4. Customize colors and text
5. Export as PDFs
6. **Your work: 4-6 hours one-time**

**OPTION C: Buy PLR** ($20-40, instant, medium uniqueness)
1. Go to Creative Fabrica or Etsy
2. Search "PLR printable planners"
3. Buy existing planner sets
4. Rebrand with your colors
5. **Your work: 1-2 hours customizing**

### Day 2: Marketing Setup (1-2 hours)

**Step 1: Create Pinterest Account** (10 minutes)
1. Sign up at pinterest.com/business
2. Set up business account (free)
3. Add your website
4. Verify your domain

**Step 2: Create First 20 Pins** (1 hour)
- Use Canva pin templates (1000x1500px)
- Show preview of your planners
- Text overlay: "Free Printable Budget Planner" → Link to your site
- Create 5 pins per pack = 20 total
- Schedule using Pinterest's built-in scheduler

**Step 3: Post in 5 Places** (30 minutes)
- Reddit: r/productivity, r/bujo, r/planners
- Facebook: Join 3 planner groups, share
- Twitter/X: "Just launched my printable planner shop!"
- Instagram: Post preview images
- Your personal network: Email/message friends

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Tip 1: Start with 1-2 Packs
Don't wait to create all 69 printables. Launch with Budget + Productivity packs (35 pages) and add the rest later. Faster to market = faster revenue.

### Tip 2: Pinterest is Your Best Friend
Spend 80% of marketing time on Pinterest. It's the #1 traffic source for printables. Other platforms are bonuses.

### Tip 3: Seasonal Opportunities
- **January**: New Year goals, budget planning (HUGE month)
- **August-September**: Back to school (student planners)
- **June**: Summer goals, wedding planning
- **November**: Next year planning, Black Friday sales

### Tip 4: Build an Email List
Add a popup: "Get 3 FREE printable planners" → collect emails → promote new products to list = 30-40% of future revenue.

### Tip 5: The First Month is the Hardest
Revenue will be slow at first ($100-300). This is NORMAL. Pinterest takes 4-6 weeks to gain traction. Stick with it. Month 3-4 is when it explodes.

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Do I really need to create 69 printables?**
A: No! Start with 20-30 (Budget + Productivity). Launch faster. Add more later based on what sells.

**Q: What if I can't use Canva?**
A: Hire on Fiverr for $30-50. Send them the specs. They do everything. You do nothing.

**Q: How long until I make my first sale?**
A: With active marketing: 1-7 days. Pinterest takes longer (2-4 weeks) but then becomes your main source.

**Q: Is $5K/month realistic?**
A: Yes, but it takes 3-6 months of consistent marketing. $1K-2K/month is more common by month 3-4.

**Q: What if I don't hit $5K?**
A: Even $1K-2K/month passive income is life-changing. Start there. Scale over time.

**Q: Can I sell on Etsy too?**
A: Yes! Upload to both Gumroad and Etsy. Double the exposure. Etsy has fees (~10%) but huge traffic.

**Q: Do I need to provide support?**
A: Minimal. It's PDFs - very straightforward. Maybe 1-2 questions per week. Include a FAQ in your delivery email.

**Q: What about Notion templates (Phase 2)?**
A: They're in the `phase2-notion` folder. Add them as a 2nd product line once printables are profitable. Diversify income.
