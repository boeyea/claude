## BUSINESS PLANNING (30 prompts)

### Prompt 1: Business Idea Validator
Act as an experienced business consultant. I'm considering starting a business in [INDUSTRY/NICHE]. Analyze this idea: [BRIEF DESCRIPTION]. Provide: 1) Market viability assessment, 2) Target audience identification, 3) Key competitors and differentiation opportunities, 4) Revenue potential, 5) Top 3 risks and mitigation strategies, 6) Actionable first steps to validate the concept.

### Prompt 2: Business Model Canvas Generator
You're a strategic business planner. Help me create a complete Business Model Canvas for [BUSINESS TYPE]. Include: 1) Customer segments, 2) Value propositions, 3) Channels, 4) Customer relationships, 5) Revenue streams, 6) Key resources, 7) Key activities, 8) Key partnerships, 9) Cost structure. Provide specific examples for each section.

### Prompt 3: Competitive Analysis Framework
Act as a market analyst with 15 years of experience. Conduct a detailed competitive analysis for my [PRODUCT/SERVICE] in the [INDUSTRY] market. Identify: 1) Top 5-7 direct competitors with their strengths/weaknesses, 2) Market positioning gaps, 3) Pricing strategies comparison, 4) Unique selling propositions analysis, 5) Opportunities for differentiation, 6) Strategic recommendations for competitive advantage.

### Prompt 4: SWOT Analysis Deep Dive
You're a strategic business advisor. Create a comprehensive SWOT analysis for [BUSINESS/PRODUCT]. For Strengths: list internal advantages and core competencies. For Weaknesses: identify areas needing improvement. For Opportunities: highlight market trends and growth potential. For Threats: analyze external risks and competitive pressures. Provide 5-7 items per category with actionable insights.

### Prompt 5: Business Plan Executive Summary
Act as a professional business plan writer. Create a compelling 1-page executive summary for [BUSINESS NAME], a [BUSINESS TYPE] targeting [TARGET MARKET]. Include: 1) Company overview and mission, 2) Problem being solved, 3) Solution/product description, 4) Market opportunity size, 5) Competitive advantage, 6) Financial highlights and projections, 7) Funding requirements (if applicable), 8) Team qualifications. Make it investor-ready.

### Prompt 6: Market Entry Strategy
You're an international business strategist. Develop a market entry strategy for [COMPANY] entering [NEW MARKET/REGION]. Analyze: 1) Market size and growth potential, 2) Regulatory requirements and barriers, 3) Cultural considerations, 4) Entry mode recommendation (partnership, acquisition, greenfield), 5) Go-to-market timeline with phases, 6) Risk assessment, 7) Success metrics and KPIs.

### Prompt 7: Value Proposition Designer
Act as a value proposition expert. Help me craft a powerful value proposition for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] targeting [CUSTOMER SEGMENT]. Answer: 1) What jobs is the customer trying to get done?, 2) What pains do they experience?, 3) What gains do they desire?, 4) How does our product/service address each pain point?, 5) How do we create gains?, 6) Write 3 versions of value proposition statements optimized for different channels.

### Prompt 8: Financial Projection Model
You're a financial analyst specializing in startups. Create detailed 3-year financial projections for [BUSINESS TYPE] with [BUSINESS MODEL]. Include: 1) Revenue projections broken down by stream, 2) Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), 3) Operating expenses (fixed and variable), 4) Cash flow projections, 5) Break-even analysis, 6) Key assumptions and drivers, 7) Best/worst case scenarios. Present in table format.

### Prompt 9: Customer Persona Creator
Act as a customer research specialist. Develop 3 detailed customer personas for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] in [INDUSTRY]. For each persona include: 1) Demographics (age, income, location, education), 2) Job title and career stage, 3) Goals and motivations, 4) Pain points and frustrations, 5) Information sources and media consumption, 6) Buying behavior and decision criteria, 7) How our product/service fits their needs, 8) Objections we need to overcome.

### Prompt 10: Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Planner
You're a lean startup advisor. Help me define an MVP for [PRODUCT IDEA] targeting [AUDIENCE]. Specify: 1) Core problem being solved, 2) Must-have features (top 5), 3) Nice-to-have features (postpone for v2), 4) Key assumptions to validate, 5) Success metrics, 6) Development timeline estimate, 7) Go-to-market approach for MVP launch, 8) Feedback collection strategy.

## HOW TO USE THESE PROMPTS

### Step 1: Copy the Prompt
Select the prompt that matches your business need and copy it.

### Step 2: Customize the Variables
Replace ALL text in [BRACKETS] with your specific details:
- [INDUSTRY/NICHE] → "SaaS software" or "E-commerce fashion"
- [TARGET AUDIENCE] → "CFOs at mid-market companies" or "millennial women"
- [PRODUCT/SERVICE] → Your specific offering

### Step 3: Paste into ChatGPT
Paste the customized prompt into ChatGPT (GPT-4 recommended for best results, but GPT-3.5 also works).

### Step 4: Refine if Needed
If the output isn't perfect, ask ChatGPT to:
- "Make it more detailed"
- "Focus on [specific aspect]"
- "Provide more examples"
- "Make it shorter/more concise"

### Pro Tips:
1. **Start with context**: Before using a prompt, tell ChatGPT about your business in 2-3 sentences
2. **Ask follow-ups**: These prompts are starting points. Ask ChatGPT to elaborate on specific sections
3. **Combine prompts**: Use multiple prompts together for comprehensive solutions
4. **Save good outputs**: Keep a doc of great ChatGPT responses for future reference
5. **Iterate**: Run the same prompt multiple times with slight variations to get different perspectives
