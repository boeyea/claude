## YOUTUBE CONTENT (40 prompts)

### 1. Viral YouTube Video Ideas Generator
Act as a YouTube strategist with multiple viral videos. Generate 20 video ideas for [NICHE/TOPIC] channel targeting [AUDIENCE]. For each idea: 1) Catchy title using proven formulas (How to, X Ways, I tried, etc.), 2) Video hook (first 10 seconds), 3) Content structure, 4) Target video length, 5) Thumbnail concept, 6) Why it will perform well, 7) Keyword/SEO angle. Mix educational, entertaining, and trending topics.

### 2. YouTube Script Writer
You're a YouTube scriptwriter for channels with 1M+ subscribers. Write a complete script for a [LENGTH] minute video about [TOPIC]. Include: 1) Cold open hook (15 seconds), 2) Intro with channel branding (15 seconds), 3) Main content sections with beats (outline timestamps), 4) Engagement reminders (like, subscribe prompts), 5) B-roll suggestions, 6) Transitions between sections, 7) Jokes or personality moments, 8) Call-to-action, 9) Outro with next video tease. Write conversationally, not formally.

### 3. YouTube Title Optimizer
Act as a YouTube SEO expert. I have a video about [TOPIC]. Create 15 title variations optimized for: 1) Click-through rate (curiosity + benefit), 2) SEO (keyword placement), 3) Algorithm recommendation. For each title: A) Include target keyword naturally, B) Use proven patterns (numbers, how-to, vs, before/after), C) Create curiosity gap, D) Include emotional trigger, E) Keep under 60 characters. Mark your top 3 picks with rationale.

### 4. YouTube Thumbnail Copy Ideas
You're a thumbnail designer who understands click psychology. For a video about [VIDEO TOPIC], create 10 thumbnail text concepts. For each: 1) Main text (3-5 words max, large and readable), 2) Secondary text if needed (smaller), 3) Emotion or facial expression to show, 4) Color scheme recommendation, 5) Visual elements to include, 6) What emotion it should evoke, 7) Why it will get clicks. Make text bold, contrasting, and curiosity-inducing.

### 5. YouTube Description Template
Act as a YouTube growth expert. Write an optimized video description template for [CHANNEL TYPE]. Include: 1) Compelling first 2 lines (before "show more"), 2) Keyword-rich video summary (150 words), 3) Timestamped chapters (format: 00:00 Intro), 4) Resources/links section, 5) Products mentioned section, 6) Social media links with CTAs, 7) Subscribe CTA with channel link, 8) Collaboration/business contact, 9) Hashtags (3-5), 10) Video tags recommendation (20-30). Front-load keywords for SEO.

### 6. YouTube Series Planning
You're a content strategist. Plan a YouTube series on [TOPIC] with 8-12 episodes. For each episode: 1) Episode title, 2) Core lesson/value, 3) Key points covered, 4) How it builds on previous episodes, 5) Cliffhanger or tease for next episode, 6) Optimal release schedule. Also provide: series name, series trailer concept, playlist description, cross-promotion strategy between episodes.

### 7. YouTube Community Post Ideas
Act as a community engagement specialist. Create 30 YouTube Community post ideas for [CHANNEL NICHE]. Include: 1) Poll questions (with 4 answer options), 2) Behind-the-scenes updates, 3) Thumbnail A/B tests, 4) Upcoming video teases, 5) Community shoutouts, 6) Quick tips or facts, 7) Memes relevant to niche, 8) Questions for engagement, 9) Channel milestones celebrations. Specify optimal posting frequency and timing.

### 8. YouTube Shorts Script Generator
You're a YouTube Shorts expert. Create 15 YouTube Shorts script ideas for [NICHE]. Each should be 15-60 seconds. For each short: 1) Hook in first 2 seconds (text overlay), 2) Quick value or entertainment, 3) Loop potential (ends where it begins), 4) On-screen text timing, 5) Music/sound suggestion, 6) Ending CTA (subscribe, watch full video, comment), 7) Hashtags. Make them vertical-format optimized and highly retainable.

### 9. YouTube Collaboration Pitch
Act as a collaboration strategist. Write an outreach message to pitch collaboration with [CREATOR NAME] in [NICHE] for my channel [YOUR CHANNEL]. Include: 1) Personalized opening (reference their content), 2) Why you're reaching out, 3) What you admire about their channel, 4) Collaboration idea (value for both audiences), 5) Your channel's value proposition (subscribers, niche, engagement), 6) Logistics and commitment, 7) Flexible options, 8) Next steps. Make it professional, concise, and compelling.

### 10. YouTube Analytics Interpretation
You're a YouTube analytics expert. I'll provide my video statistics [PROVIDE STATS]. Analyze: 1) CTR performance (good/bad for niche), 2) AVD% and retention curve insights, 3) Traffic sources analysis, 4) Audience demographics takeaways, 5) Outperforming vs underperforming videos patterns, 6) What's working, 7) What to improve, 8) Content recommendations, 9) Upload schedule optimization. Give actionable insights.

## BLOG & ARTICLE WRITING (40 prompts)

### 11. Blog Post Idea Generator
Act as a content marketing strategist. Generate 50 blog post ideas for [BLOG NICHE] targeting [AUDIENCE]. Organize by content type: 1) How-to guides (10 ideas), 2) Listicles (10 ideas), 3) Case studies (5 ideas), 4) Comparison posts (5 ideas), 5) Opinion pieces (5 ideas), 6) Data-driven posts (5 ideas), 7) Ultimate guides (5 ideas), 8) FAQs (5 ideas). For each: working title, target keyword, search intent, estimated traffic potential.

### 12. SEO Blog Post Outline Creator
You're an SEO content planner. Create a comprehensive outline for a blog post targeting "[KEYWORD]" (search volume: [X], difficulty: [Y]). Include: 1) SEO-optimized title (60 chars), 2) Meta description (155 chars), 3) Introduction (what you'll cover), 4) H2 and H3 structure (10-15 headings with keywords), 5) Key points under each heading, 6) Internal links to suggest (5-7), 7) External authority links (3-5), 8) Content gaps vs top-ranking pages, 9) FAQ section (5-8 questions), 10) Conclusion with CTA.

### 13. Listicle Article Writer
Act as a listicle specialist. Write a complete listicle article: "[NUMBER] [TOPIC] That [BENEFIT]" (e.g., "15 Productivity Tools That Save 10 Hours/Week"). Include: 1) Engaging introduction (why this list matters, 150 words), 2) For each list item: catchy subheading, description (100-150 words), why it's included, pro tip or example, 3) Comparison table summarizing all items, 4) Conclusion with key takeaway, 5) FAQ section, 6) Related articles suggestions. Make it skimmable with bullets, bold text, and clear structure.

### 14. How-To Guide Template
You're a tutorial content expert. Write a detailed how-to guide: "How to [ACHIEVE RESULT]" for [AUDIENCE]. Structure: 1) Introduction (problem and why guide is needed, 200 words), 2) What you'll need (tools, materials, prerequisites), 3) Step-by-step instructions (7-15 steps with descriptions), 4) Tips for success (5-7 tips), 5) Common mistakes to avoid (3-5), 6) Troubleshooting section, 7) FAQ (6-8 questions), 8) Conclusion and next steps. Include visuals suggestions (screenshots, diagrams) for each step.

### 15. Case Study Article Framework
Act as a case study writer. Write a compelling case study: "How [COMPANY/PERSON] [ACHIEVED RESULT] Using [METHOD/PRODUCT]". Include: 1) Attention-grabbing introduction with result upfront, 2) Background (who they are, starting situation), 3) Challenge or problem (detailed pain points, 200 words), 4) Solution implementation (what they did, 300 words), 5) Results with specific metrics (before/after comparison), 6) Key takeaways and lessons (5-7 bullets), 7) What readers can apply, 8) CTA. Use quotes throughout to add authenticity.

### 16. Product Review Article
You're a product review expert. Write an in-depth review of [PRODUCT NAME] in [CATEGORY]. Include: 1) Quick verdict (TL;DR at top), 2) Product overview (what it is, who it's for), 3) Unboxing/first impressions, 4) Key features breakdown (5-7 features with analysis), 5) Performance testing (specific tests conducted, results), 6) Pros and cons list, 7) Comparison with 2-3 alternatives, 8) Who should buy this (ideal customer), 9) Who should skip it, 10) Final rating and verdict, 11) Where to buy (affiliate links), 12) FAQ. 1500-2000 words.

### 17. Ultimate Guide Structure
Act as a pillar content creator. Create an "Ultimate Guide to [TOPIC]" structure for comprehensive coverage. Provide: 1) Guide title, 2) Introduction (why this guide exists, 300 words), 3) Table of contents (15-20 sections), 4) For each section: heading, key points to cover, word count target, 5) Subsections with H3 headings, 6) Expert quotes or data to include, 7) Visual content suggestions (infographics, charts, videos), 8) Internal linking strategy, 9) Lead magnet or content upgrade idea, 10) Promotion strategy. Total target: 4000-6000 words.

### 18. Comparison Article Builder
You're a comparison content specialist. Write a comparison article: "[PRODUCT A] vs [PRODUCT B]: Which is Better for [USE CASE]?" Include: 1) Introduction (why this comparison matters), 2) Quick comparison table (features, pricing, ratings), 3) Overview of each product (150 words each), 4) Head-to-head comparison across 6-8 criteria with winner per category, 5) Pros and cons for each, 6) Pricing comparison, 7) Use case recommendations (when to choose each), 8) Alternatives to consider, 9) Final verdict and recommendation, 10) FAQ addressing common comparison questions.

### 19. Storytelling Blog Post
Act as a narrative writer. Write a personal story blog post about [EXPERIENCE/TOPIC] that teaches [LESSON]. Structure: 1) Hook opening (provocative or interesting moment), 2) Set the scene (context, where/when), 3) Rising action (challenge, conflict, journey), 4) Climax (turning point), 5) Resolution (what happened), 6) Reflection (what you learned), 7) How readers can apply this lesson, 8) Call-to-action. Make it vivid with sensory details, dialogue, and emotion. 1000-1500 words.

### 20. Data-Driven Article Creator
You're a data journalism expert. Write a data-driven article: "[SURPRISING STATISTIC] About [TOPIC]: What the Data Tells Us". Include: 1) Attention-grabbing headline with main stat, 2) Executive summary, 3) Data collection methodology, 4) Key findings (5-7 insights with supporting data), 5) Data visualization descriptions (charts, graphs needed), 6) Analysis and interpretation, 7) Industry expert quotes, 8) Implications for readers, 9) Predictions or trends, 10) Conclusion with actionable takeaways, 11) Data sources cited, 12) Related statistics. Make data accessible and engaging.
