Frequently Asked Questions

How does delivery work?

Immediately after purchase, you'll receive an email with download links to your PDF files. Download and save them to your computer or cloud storage.

Can I print these multiple times?

Yes! Once you purchase, you can print unlimited copies for personal use. Print a fresh copy whenever you need one.

What size paper do I need?

All planners come in both US Letter (8.5" x 11") and A4 formats, so you can use whatever paper you have!

Can I edit the PDFs?

Yes! Most planners have fillable fields where you can type directly into the PDF before printing. Or print blank and handwrite.

What if I don't have a printer?

No problem! You can take the PDF files to any print shop (Staples, FedEx Office, local shops) and they'll print them for you inexpensively.

Is there a refund policy?

Yes! 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not happy with your purchase, email us for a full refund.
